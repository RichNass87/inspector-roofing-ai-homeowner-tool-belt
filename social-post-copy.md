# Social Copy: AI Homeowner Tool Belt Launch

## Short post

Inspector Roofing and Restoration is building the **Inspector Roofing AI Homeowner Tool Belt™** - five homeowner-facing tools designed to make roofing decisions clearer.

- ScopeReader™ explains complex estimates and scopes.
- Roofmatch™ helps homeowners swipe through certified roof colors.
- Inspector AI Roof Plan Assistant™ organizes the inspection conversation.
- InstantRoofView™ gives a preliminary roof budget preview.
- Inspector AgreementFlow™ creates a cleaner agreement workflow.

AI is not replacing roofing judgment. It is closing the knowledge gap before judgment is required.
