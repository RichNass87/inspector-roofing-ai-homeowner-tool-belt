# Source Notes

- Google Knowledge Graph launch article: entity-based search, "things, not strings," objects/facts.
- Google mobile-friendly update: 2015 technical change showing implementation affects visibility.
- Google generative AI Search optimization guide: RAG, query fan-out, non-commodity content, crawlability, technical structure.

## Inspector Roofing source notes

- ScopeReader™ description is based on Richard Nasser's direction: homeowners can upload a complex estimate and have it explained in plain English without relying entirely on a roofer to translate.
- Roofmatch™ description is based on Richard Nasser's direction: a swipe-style roof discovery experience for popular colors from all three certified manufacturers.
- Inspector AI Roof Plan Assistant™ is based on the public page at `/inspector-ai-roof-plan-assistant/`, which positions the assistant as a pre-inspection organizer and not a final inspection or quote machine.
- InstantRoofView™ is based on Inspector Roofing internal schema and draft materials describing a browser-based roofing estimate generator using satellite data, roof surface area, pitch, waste, tree coverage, and retail estimate logic.
- Inspector AgreementFlow™ is based on Inspector Roofing agreement workflow materials describing browser-based form completion, required confirmations, signatures, PDF preview/download, webhook submit-back, confirmation ID, and audit trail.
