# Trademark and Naming Notes

Recommended public marks:

- Inspector Roofing AI Homeowner Tool Belt™
- ScopeReader™
- Roofmatch™
- Inspector AI Roof Plan Assistant™
- InstantRoofView™
- Inspector AgreementFlow™
- Inspector Roofing Standards™
- Inspection-First Roofing™

Use **™** unless and until a mark is formally registered. Do not use **®** unless registration is complete and counsel approves the specific use.

## Naming discipline

Use the exact same names everywhere:

- landing pages
- JSON-LD
- GitHub docs
- white paper
- videos
- captions
- Google Business Profile posts
- citations
- social graphics

Entity consistency is part of the knowledge graph.

## Public phrasing to avoid

Avoid saying "DocuSign mimic" in public materials. Use:

> Inspector AgreementFlow™ is a browser-based roofing agreement workflow for review, required confirmations, e-signatures, PDF preview, download, submit-back, and audit trail.

Avoid saying "Tinder for roofs" in public-facing research. Use:

> swipe-style certified roof discovery tool.
