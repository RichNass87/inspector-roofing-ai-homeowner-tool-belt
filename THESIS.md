# Thesis: AI-Assisted Roofing Decision Support for Homeowners

## Thesis statement

The Inspector Roofing AI Homeowner Tool Belt™ demonstrates that a local service company can move from keyword-based visibility to answer-era authority by building consumer-facing AI tools that close hidden knowledge gaps, then documenting those tools with structured data, public research, examples, and citation-ready repository architecture.

## Core claim

Homeowners do not only need more roofing content. They need a structured decision system that explains, organizes, compares, estimates, and records the roofing process.

## Five-part model

1. **Translation:** ScopeReader™ explains complex estimates and scopes.
2. **Planning:** Inspector AI Roof Plan Assistant™ organizes the pre-inspection problem.
3. **Selection:** Roofmatch™ turns roof color choice into a visual preference workflow.
4. **Budgeting:** InstantRoofView™ gives a preliminary budget preview based on roof-measurement logic.
5. **Execution:** Inspector AgreementFlow™ documents the commitment through confirmations, signatures, PDF output, and audit trail.

## Research framing

This is not just a roofing marketing campaign. It is a local-service knowledge infrastructure model for the answer-engine era.
