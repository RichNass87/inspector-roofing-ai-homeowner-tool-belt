# Safety Boundaries

## System-level boundary

AI explains and organizes. Inspector Roofing verifies, inspects, documents, scopes, prices, and installs.

## ScopeReader™

ScopeReader™ is for plain-language educational explanation. It should not claim to provide:

- legal advice
- insurance coverage advice
- claim approval guarantees
- final construction scope decisions
- policy interpretation
- public adjuster services

## Roofmatch™

Roofmatch™ helps homeowners explore roof colors and certified manufacturer options. It should not claim final availability, exact color rendering, manufacturer endorsement, or warranty eligibility unless independently verified.

## Inspector AI Roof Plan Assistant™

The assistant is a pre-inspection planning tool. It does not replace physical inspection, professional judgment, or final measurement.

## InstantRoofView™

InstantRoofView™ provides a preliminary budget preview. Final pricing requires inspection, final measurement, site-condition review, code review, and scope confirmation.

## Inspector AgreementFlow™

AgreementFlow™ requires legal review before production use. Electronic-signature language, consumer consent, audit trail, record retention, and state-specific requirements should be reviewed by counsel.
