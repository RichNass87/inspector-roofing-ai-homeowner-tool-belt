# Consumer Knowledge Gap Map

| Gap | Homeowner symptom | Tool | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Translation | "I do not understand this estimate." | ScopeReader™ | Plain-English explanation and question list. |
| Planning | "I do not know what to prepare before inspection." | Inspector AI Roof Plan Assistant™ | Organized inspection prep and documentation checklist. |
| Selection | "I cannot choose a roof color." | Roofmatch™ | Swipe-based preference clarity. |
| Budget | "I need a price range before I talk to someone." | InstantRoofView™ | Preliminary budget preview. |
| Execution | "I need to sign and keep a clean record." | Inspector AgreementFlow™ | Signed PDF, audit trail, submit-back workflow. |

## Why these gaps are hard to see

Homeowners often think they have a roofing problem when they actually have a knowledge-sequence problem. They need the right information at the right moment.

The tool belt turns roofing uncertainty into a step-by-step decision support system.
