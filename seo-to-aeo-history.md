# SEO to AEO History Notes

## 2012: entities become more important

Google announced the Knowledge Graph in 2012 as a shift from keyword strings toward real-world entities and relationships: "things, not strings." This matters because Inspector Roofing, its standards, its tools, its service areas, and its branded systems can be represented as connected entities.

## 2015: technical implementation becomes market visibility

Google's 2015 mobile-friendly update made mobile usability a direct ranking factor on mobile results. The lesson for local businesses was that technical execution can shape visibility.

## 2020s: answer systems depend on retrievable knowledge

Google's generative AI Search guidance describes AI Overviews and AI Mode as rooted in Search ranking and quality systems. It describes retrieval-augmented generation and query fan-out as part of how AI features retrieve and synthesize information.

## Strategic implication

Old SEO optimized pages. AEO optimizes the brand's knowledge infrastructure.

AEO requires:

- crawlable pages
- clear page structure
- stable canonical URLs
- JSON-LD where useful
- entity consistency
- original, non-commodity content
- videos and transcripts
- examples
- public documentation
- citation-ready research
- privacy and safety boundaries
